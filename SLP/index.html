FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all products at Sri Lakshmi Plaza genuine and original?

Yes, absolutely! We are an authorized retailer for all major brands including Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Google, and more. Every product comes with the manufacturer's official warranty and all required documentation.

Do you offer EMI options for phone purchases?

Yes! We offer No Cost EMI options on all major credit cards from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, and more. We also have partnerships with Bajaj Finance and other NBFCs for flexible financing options starting from 3 to 24 months.

What is your exchange/trade-in policy?

We offer a transparent trade-in program for your old phone. Bring your old device to our store and our experts will evaluate it and give you the best market value. The trade-in amount is deducted directly from your new phone purchase.

Do you offer phone repair services?

Yes! We have a dedicated repair center with certified technicians. We handle screen replacements, battery replacements, water damage repair, software issues, and more. Most repairs are completed within 2-4 hours.

What are your store hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on major public holidays. You can also reach us on WhatsApp for quick inquiries.

Do you provide home delivery?

Yes, we offer same-day delivery within Vijayawada city limits and next-day delivery across Krishna and Guntur districts. Free delivery is available on orders above ₹15,000. Please WhatsApp us or call to arrange delivery.

Can I test phones before buying?

Absolutely! Our showroom has fully operational demo units of all featured phones. Our knowledgeable staff will help you experience the phones hands-on and answer all your questions before you make a purchase decision.

Do you have festival offers and special discounts?

Yes! We regularly run festival offers during Dussehra, Diwali, Christmas, and New Year. We also offer special student discounts, cashback offers on select bank cards, and exclusive exchange bonus deals throughout the year.
