📖 Our Story

Built on Trust & Excellence

Sri Lakshmi Plaza was founded with a single vision: to bring the best mobile technology experience to the people of Vijayawada. What started as a small phone shop in Labbipet has grown into the city's most trusted premium mobile phone destination.

Over a decade of serving thousands of happy customers, we've built our reputation on three pillars: genuine products, transparent pricing, and exceptional service.

Today, we are authorized retailers for all major smartphone brands and carry 500+ products — from flagship iPhones and Samsung Galaxy to budget-friendly options. Our expert team of 20+ professionals is always ready to help you find the perfect device.
